New · Dreaming

Moxie dreams
on your meetings.

When your Mac is idle and on power, Moxie reads your meetings again. Each day you get up to three new cards, a recap of yesterday and what is still open. Every card cites the transcript lines.

  • Promises, both ways.

    What you owe, and what others owe you.

  • Topics that come back.

    Recurring topics and blockers, for each person.

  • Connections.

    Two people raised the same thing in different meetings.

  • Dropped threads.

    Open items, moved dates and people you lost touch with.

Dismiss a card, ask about it or draft a follow-up. Turn Dreaming off, or leave out a note or a folder. It all stays on your Mac.
