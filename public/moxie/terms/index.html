Moxie terms of use

Last updated 19 November 2026.

These terms apply when you download or use Moxie, the meeting-notes app made by Volantis Labs ("we"). If you do not agree, do not use Moxie. Questions: hello@getmoxieapp.com.

1. What Moxie does

Moxie records the sound of meetings on your computer, transcribes it, labels speakers and writes notes and summaries. All of this happens on your computer. We do not receive your meeting content. The privacy policy says what Moxie sends and when.

2. Your recordings and the law

You are responsible for using Moxie lawfully. In many places, the law requires you to tell people, or get their consent, before you record or transcribe a conversation. Moxie reminds you, gives you a notice line you can copy into the meeting, and adds a record to each note. You decide whether and how to use them. Do not use Moxie to record people in secret or against the law.

3. Notes made by a language model

Transcripts, speaker names and summaries are made automatically and can be wrong. Check important facts against the transcript before you rely on them. Each summary statement links to the transcript for this reason.

4. Google and Microsoft accounts

When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, Moxie reads your calendar, read only, from your computer. Their own terms also apply to your account. You can disconnect in Moxie's Settings, or revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account at any time.

5. Your data

Your notes are yours. They are stored on your computer, and you are responsible for backups. If you delete a note forever or uninstall Moxie, we cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.

6. Updates

Moxie checks for updates and installs them between meetings, never during a capture. Some features may change or be removed as the app develops.
