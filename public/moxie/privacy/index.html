Moxie privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. Applies to the Moxie app for Mac, made by Volantis Labs.

Questions, or requests for a copy or deletion of your data: hello@getmoxieapp.com. We reply within one month.

Meeting content stays on your Mac

Audio, transcripts, notes, summaries, speaker names, calendar data and screen text are never sent to Moxie. Transcription, speaker attribution and summaries run locally.

Meeting audio is kept only until the note is processed and is then deleted. If processing fails, the audio is kept so it can be retried, until processing succeeds or you delete the note forever. No audio is stored with a note.

Google and Microsoft sign-in and calendar

Sign in with Google or Microsoft to connect your calendar, read only. Moxie reads your email address and event titles, times, attendees and meeting links for upcoming meetings, countdowns, note titles and speaker-name suggestions. Calendar events stay on your Mac, never with Moxie or PostHog. They are never sold or used for ads.

Google access uses the read-only calendar events and calendar list scopes, plus sign-in identity (openid and email). Moxie stores on your Mac the account email, calendar list, event titles, start and end times, attendee names and email addresses, and meeting links. Google also returns event descriptions and locations; Moxie does not store those fields. Microsoft calendars use read-only access and the same stored fields.

Moxie's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Diagnostics

Regions that ask first are the EU (including its outermost regions and Åland), EEA, UK, Switzerland, Gibraltar, Jersey, Guernsey, the Isle of Man, Canada, South Korea and China. We also ask first if your Mac's region cannot be read. In these regions, your email address never leaves your Mac. Elsewhere, with diagnostics on, your account email address is linked to your install ID in PostHog EU to understand usage per owner and contact you about the beta. With diagnostics off, nothing is sent, including your email address.

Usage diagnostics include a random install ID, device and app details, counts, timings, error codes and fixed choices, never meeting content. Daily diagnostics include activity totals, and storage and security status. Management checks report yes/no answers, never company names, domains or tokens.

Crash reports include technical details and stack frames, without error messages or breadcrumbs. Raw crash dumps are processed locally and then deleted.

Usage events use PostHog EU and are kept for 12 months. Crash reports use Sentry EU and are kept for 30 days. Your email address is kept until you ask for deletion or 90 days after the beta ends. Diagnostics are never sold.

Local diagnostics history does not expire automatically. Turning diagnostics off discards queued events. Turning them off or resetting the install ID does not erase local history or reports already sent.

Turn off diagnostics in Settings under Your data. "What was sent" shows recorded sending attempts, including ones that may not have arrived. In regions that ask first, nothing is sent before you agree. Elsewhere diagnostics are on by default. Managed policy can force them off.

Other network use

Model downloads, update checks and calendar access use the network. You review GitHub issue reports before submitting. The optional team contact form sends your work email, company name and consent time separately; withdraw that consent in Settings.

Your choices

Email us to ask for a copy or deletion of your data. Deletion removes your PostHog person record.

To sign out, disconnect all accounts in Settings under General, in the calendar section. You can also revoke access on the Google Account connections page, the Microsoft account app permissions page, or for a managed Microsoft account, My Apps.

Deleting a meeting forever deletes the meeting from Moxie. Files you exported, Moxie backups and Time Machine copies are not touched.
